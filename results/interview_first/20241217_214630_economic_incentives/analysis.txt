# Title: Economic Incentives for Young Couples
# Experiment description: We explore the impact of economic incentives and job security measures on marriage and childbirth rates among young professionals in Japan. This policy includes financial subsidies for housing, a child allowance, affordable childcare, and flexible work hours. Additionally, a public awareness campaign will inform young people about these benefits. We conduct interviews with different personas to gauge the potential impact and feasibility of these policies.
