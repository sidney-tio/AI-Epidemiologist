# Title: Promoting Lifelong Learning and Continuous Professional Development in Japan
# Experiment description: We will explore the feasibility and impact of policies aimed at promoting lifelong learning and continuous professional development among Japan's workforce. Through interviews with educators, industry leaders, policymakers, and workers, we will identify the key barriers and opportunities for lifelong learning. We will propose targeted policies such as subsidies for continuing education, tax incentives for companies that invest in employee training, and public-private partnerships to create accessible learning platforms. Additionally, we will consider specific initiatives for underrepresented groups such as women re-entering the workforce and older adults. Digital learning platforms will be emphasized to increase accessibility and flexibility for workers. We will also implement metrics and success indicators to evaluate the impact of the policies, such as tracking the number of participants in lifelong learning programs, the employment rates of those who undergo continuous professional development, and feedback from both employees and employers.
