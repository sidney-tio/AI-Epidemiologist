# Title: Environmental Sustainability: Impact of Policy Interventions on Carbon Footprint and Recycling Behavior
# Experiment description: In this experiment, we will simulate a society where agents have attributes such as carbon footprint and recycling behavior. Policies like carbon taxes, subsidies for green products, and public awareness campaigns will be implemented. Necessary modifications include adding 'carbon_footprint' and 'recycling_behavior' attributes to the Agent class, defining how these attributes influence agent behavior, and creating functions to simulate the policies. Results will be measured through metrics like average carbon footprint, recycling rates, and overall environmental impact. Effectiveness of the policies will be analyzed by comparing these metrics before and after policy implementation.

## Agent Results Plot (agent_results.png)
This plot shows the evolution of various agent-level metrics over time for each run. The x-axis represents the timesteps, and the y-axis represents the average value of the metric across all agents. Each line represents a different run, as indicated by the legend. The metrics plotted include:
- Wealth: The average wealth of agents.
- Steps_not_given: The average number of steps agents did not give money.
- CarbonFootprint: The average carbon footprint of agents.
- RecyclingBehavior: The average recycling behavior of agents.
