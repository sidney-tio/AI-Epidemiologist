In terms of services, the Head Start program in the United States is a model for accessible, high-quality early childhood education. The program provides comprehensive, developmentally appropriate care for low-income children, along with services for their families. 

Lastly, the Healthy Homes Program in New Zealand is another example. This initiative focuses on improving housing conditions for low-income families, with an emphasis on creating warm, dry, and safe homes. This program has been linked to improved health outcomes for children, including lower rates of hospitalization.

Each of these examples highlights the importance of taking a comprehensive, community-focused approach to creating family-friendly urban environments. Interviewee: Continuous improvement and practical implementation of Family-Friendly Urban Zones require a commitment to ongoing evaluation, adaptation, and community engagement.

Another strategy is to use a "Place-Based" approach, which focuses on improving outcomes in specific geographic areas by addressing the unique needs and leveraging the unique assets of each community.
